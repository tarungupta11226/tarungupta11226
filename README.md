## About Me

I’m interested in the intersection of **machine learning, artificial intelligence, mathematics, scientific research, robotics, and software development**.

I like learning by building. My projects have ranged from machine learning applications and data-driven tools to **local LLM/RAG systems, maritime software, forecasting, and robotics projects**. I enjoy taking an idea from a concept into something that can actually be tested, improved, and used.

Research is another major part of what I want to explore. My current work looks at **symbolic regression for interpretable indicators of chaotic systems**, investigating whether complex quantities such as Lyapunov exponents and correlation dimension can be approximated with simpler mathematical expressions.

Alongside technical work, I’m a **co-founder of my school's Robotics Club**, where I teach younger students through hands-on Arduino, electronics, and robotics projects. Teaching has become an important part of how I learn because explaining a concept clearly forces me to understand it more deeply.

I’m currently strengthening my foundations in **machine learning, deep learning, mathematics for AI, C++, and competitive programming**, while continuing to work on research, software, and applied AI projects.
