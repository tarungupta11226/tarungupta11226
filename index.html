Hi! I'm a high school student in Mumbai, currently pursuing the IBDP at Chatrabhuj Narsee School. I enjoy working at the intersection of machine learning, scientific research, robotics, and software, especially when an idea can move beyond theory and become something I can actually build or test.

Most of my work starts with a question. I've built applied AI and software projects, worked with real-world data, and explored systems such as local LLMs and retrieval-augmented assistants. I'm currently working as an Applied AI Intern at GenZab, where I'm getting hands-on experience with how AI products are evaluated and improved for real users.

I'm also interested in research. My current work explores symbolic regression for interpretable indicators of chaotic systems, with the broader goal of finding mathematical ideas that can be useful without becoming unnecessarily complicated. Alongside this, I co-founded my school's Robotics Club, where I teach younger students through hands-on Arduino and electronics projects.

I like learning by building, but I also care about understanding what is happening underneath the code. Whether it is an ML model, a research experiment, a robotics project, or a small software tool, I usually want to know why it works, how it can be tested, and how it can be made useful.
